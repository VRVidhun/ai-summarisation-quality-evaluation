    Taxonomy of AI Summarization Errors & Omissions

    An AI summary is defined as much by what it leaves out as what it includes. Our research categorizes omissions into Critical Omissions (altering medical safety, legal compliance, or causality) and Minor Omissions (contextual details, secondary dates, and non-essential figures).

    💡
    The "Positive Reporting Bias" Law of AI Summarization: When LLMs are not strictly constrained to preserve negative findings, they suffer from an inherent positivity bias. They prioritize breakthrough metrics (e.g., 55% LDL-C drop) while systematically dropping adverse event context (e.g., patient heart attacks) or technical chokepoints (e.g., 1,150°C ceramic sintering micro-cracking).
